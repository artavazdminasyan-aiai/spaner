    How to play Spaner

    Start with one king. Recruit an army. Checkmate the other king to win. If nobody wins in 15 minutes, the bigger army wins.

    Turns & clocks

    • White starts with a king on e1, Black with a king on e8. Everything else is empty. White moves first.
    • One shared 15-minute game clock. Each turn — move, commander order and placing recruits — must finish within 30 seconds or you lose.

    New pieces appear

    • The first neutral (teal) piece appears after 10 seconds on a random empty square, any type except a king.
    • Every 10 s at first → every 30 s once someone owns 5 pieces → every 60 s once both own 10. Slower rates never speed back up.
    • Neutral pieces never move and never attack, but they do block lines.

    Recruiting

    • Capture a neutral or enemy piece and it joins you. Your piece takes its square; you place the recruit on any empty square in your half (the four rows nearest you).
    • If your half is full, place it anywhere empty. A recruit can give check the moment it lands — and it can block a check on your own king.
    • Kings can never be recruited.

    The pieces

    No castling, no two-square pawn move, no en passant. Only knights and dragons jump.

    Commander orders

    • After every five turns you complete while owning a commander, your rooks and bananas each advance one square: front pieces first, then a→h.
    • Empty square ahead → step forward. Neutral or enemy piece (not a king) → step forward and recruit it. Own piece, enemy king or edge → stay.
    • The badge on a commander shows how many of your turns remain until its next order.

    Winning

    • Checkmate wins at once. No legal turn while safe = draw.
    • At 15:00 every owned piece scores 1 (kings and banana copies included). Higher score wins, even if in check.
